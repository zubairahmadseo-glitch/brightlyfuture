What We Look For

Every article is reviewed against these standards before it is accepted.

Your Article Must Be

  • 100% original and unpublished anywhere else, including your own blog
  • At least 1,500 words of genuinely useful, practical advice
  • Based on your own experience, tests or results
  • Real data, screenshots or examples to support your points
  • Clear structure with H2 and H3 headings and short paragraphs
  • Sources linked for any statistics or claims

We Do Not Accept

  • Content generated by AI and pasted without real editing or experience
  • Articles already published on another website
  • Thin, generic or keyword-stuffed content
  • Purely promotional posts or advertorials
  • Topics on gambling, adult content, CBD, crypto schemes or loans
  • Paid links, link swaps or hidden affiliate links
Our promise to readers: every article on BrightlyFuture should help someone solve a real problem. If your article would not help you, it will not help our readers either.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about writing for BrightlyFuture.

Do you pay for guest posts?

No. We do not pay contributors at the moment, and we do not charge for publishing either. Articles are accepted purely on quality.

Can I include links in my article?

You can include one link to your website or profile in your author bio. Links inside the article are only allowed when they genuinely help readers, and our editors may remove any link that looks promotional.

Can I use AI to write my article?

You can use AI tools for research or outlining, but the final article must be written, edited and fact-checked by you, and based on your own experience. Unedited AI content will be rejected.

How long does the review take?

We usually reply to pitches within 7 to 10 working days. If you do not hear back in 2 weeks, your idea was not a fit this time.

Will my article be edited?

Yes. We may edit for clarity, structure, grammar, SEO and accuracy. For larger changes, we will check with you first.

Can I republish my article elsewhere?

No. Once published on BrightlyFuture, the article should not be republished elsewhere. You are welcome to share the link on social media and your own website.
