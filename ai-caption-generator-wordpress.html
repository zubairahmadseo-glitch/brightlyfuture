5 Expert Tips for Writing Social Media Captions That Get Engagement

Use these strategies alongside our AI caption generator for maximum results.

1

Hook Readers in the First Line

Instagram shows only the first 125 characters before the "more" link. TikTok captions get scanned in under 2 seconds. Put your strongest hook, question, or bold statement first. Our AI caption generator front-loads every caption with an attention-grabbing opener.

2

Match the Tone to Your Platform and Audience

LinkedIn audiences respond to professional insights and data. Instagram users prefer casual, relatable content. TikTok rewards humour and authenticity. Use our 7 tone options to generate captions that feel native to each platform instead of copy-pasting the same text everywhere.

3

Use 3 to 5 Hashtags on Instagram, Not 30

Instagram's own guidance recommends 3 to 5 highly relevant hashtags. Using 30 random hashtags looks spammy and does not improve reach. Our hashtag generator picks topic-relevant hashtags that align with your content rather than generic high-volume tags.

4

Always Include a Call-to-Action

Posts with CTAs get significantly more engagement than those without. Ask a question, invite comments, encourage saves, or direct followers to your link in bio. Enable the CTA option in our tool to automatically add platform-appropriate calls to action.

5

Generate Multiple Variations and A/B Test

The best social media managers test different caption styles to see what resonates with their audience. Generate 5 to 10 variations, pick 2 to 3 favourites, and test them across different posting times. Data-driven caption selection outperforms guesswork every time.
