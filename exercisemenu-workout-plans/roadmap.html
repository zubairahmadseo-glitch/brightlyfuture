How to Rank the /workout-plans/ Page & Individual Plans

Strategy 01

Hub Page SEO — Make /workout-plans/ Rank for "Workout Plans" (22.6K/mo)

The hub page targets "free workout plans", "workout plans", "workout routines" — broad terms. It ranks because it links to ALL 100 individual plans. Like LoadMuscle's hub ranks #2-3 for "workout routines" (458K/mo) by linking to 92 plans. More plans = more topical authority = higher hub ranking. Add each plan to the hub as it's published. Use CollectionPage schema. The hub page becomes the single most linked-to page on the site (every plan links back to it).

Strategy 02

Individual Plan SEO — Long-Tail Keywords Win

Each plan targets a specific long-tail keyword (e.g., "push pull legs workout plan" not "workout plan"). These long-tail terms have lower competition but high intent — searchers want THAT specific plan. Write 2,500+ words for each plan. Use HowTo schema + FAQ schema (5-7 FAQs per plan targeting related long-tail queries). Include a printable version/PDF download option.

Strategy 03

Content Depth — Beat LoadMuscle's Thin Content

LoadMuscle's 92 plans are ~500 words each. Our 100 plans at 2,500+ words = 5× deeper. Google's Helpful Content Update rewards comprehensive, original content. Each plan should include: full exercise lists with sets/reps/rest, progression models, deload protocols, exercise substitutions, nutrition guidelines, FAQ section, and printable schedule. This depth gap is our primary competitive advantage.

Strategy 04

Internal Linking Power — 500+ Bidirectional Links

Every plan links to 5+ existing articles. Every existing article links to 3+ new plans. Hub page links to all 100 plans. Plans cross-link to related plans (e.g., PPL links to UL Split as alternative). This creates 500+ internal links forming a dense topical cluster. Google uses internal links to understand site structure and distribute authority. Sites with strong internal linking rank 40% better on average.

Strategy 05

E-E-A-T Signals — Expert Authority

Every plan should include: Author byline (FISAF-certified coach), "Reviewed by" credit (registered dietitian for nutrition sections), last updated date, methodology section explaining the science behind the programming, references to research studies where applicable. These signals matter for YMYL (Your Money Your Life) fitness content.

Strategy 06

Schema Markup — Rich Results

Every plan page: HowTo schema (for the workout steps), FAQ schema (5-7 FAQs), Breadcrumb schema (Home → Workout Plans → [Category] → [Plan]). Hub page: CollectionPage schema, FAQ schema (10 FAQs), Breadcrumb schema. This earns rich snippets in Google Search, increasing CTR by 20-30%.
