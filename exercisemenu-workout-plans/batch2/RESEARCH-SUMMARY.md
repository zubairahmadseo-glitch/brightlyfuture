## What the competitors miss (gaps our pages cover)
- **Chest:** most list exercises without a weekly plan or progression; few explain why 30° incline (Rodríguez-Ridao 2020, Chaves 2020). Ours: 4 versions (2 gym, dumbbell, no equipment), 4-week progression, how to fit chest into PPL/upper-lower/bro split.
- **Arm:** competitors give one session; almost none count indirect sets from rows/presses or cite overhead triceps research (Maeo 2023) or curls vs rows (Mannarino 2021). Ours: 2 sessions/week, 4 versions including bands.
- **Core:** competitor pages are exercise lists (planks, crunches, Russian twists). None organise by core function or explain how to progress a plank. Ours: 5 core functions, progression table, spot-reduction evidence (Vispute 2011), 10-minute version.
- **Glute:** competitors lean on "activation" and kickbacks; few use MRI data. Ours: hip thrust vs squat MRI study (Plotkin 2023), full vs half squat (Kubo 2019), gluteus medius work, home version.
- **Leg day:** most give one workout. Ours: beginner, quad, hamstring and dumbbell days, exercise order table, why squats miss hamstrings and rectus femoris (Kubo 2019), seated vs lying curl (Maeo 2021), calf stretch reps (Kassiano 2023).
