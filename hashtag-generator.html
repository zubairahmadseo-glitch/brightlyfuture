What Is a Hashtag Generator

A hashtag generator is a tool that takes a topic, keyword or phrase and produces a curated list of relevant hashtags for social media platforms. Instead of guessing which tags to add to a post, you get a ready made mix of popular, medium and niche hashtags built around your subject. This tool works entirely in your browser, drawing on a hashtag database covering 24 content niches, so results appear instantly with no waiting and no account needed.

Why Hashtags Matter for Social Media Growth

Hashtags are the search engine of social media. They group content by topic so people who are interested in a subject can find your posts even if they do not already follow you. Used well, hashtags increase reach beyond your existing audience, improve discoverability in explore and search feeds, and drive engagement from users who are actively searching or scrolling that topic. The wrong hashtags, or none at all, can leave great content invisible to anyone outside your current followers.

Hashtag Strategy by Platform

Instagram

Instagram supports up to 30 hashtags per post. A mix of 8 to 15 hashtags spanning popular and niche tiers tends to perform better than maxing out the limit with only broad tags.

Twitter/X

Keep it to 1 or 2 hashtags on Twitter/X. Tweets with too many hashtags often see lower engagement, so choose the most relevant, trending tag for the conversation.

TikTok

TikTok rewards a blend of a couple of broad discovery hashtags with a few niche or trending ones. Shorter tags are easier to read on video overlays.

LinkedIn

Use 3 to 5 professional, industry specific hashtags at the end of your post. Overloading LinkedIn posts with tags can look unpolished in a professional feed.

YouTube

YouTube allows up to 15 hashtags, though only the first 3 show above your video title. Favour descriptive, searchable tags over generic ones.

How to Choose the Right Hashtags

The strongest hashtag sets combine relevance with a spread of competition levels. Relevance means every tag should genuinely describe your content, not just chase volume. Competition balance means mixing a few high volume popular tags for reach with medium tags for steady discovery and niche tags where you can realistically rank and get seen by an engaged, specific audience. Research your niche regularly since trending hashtags shift, and rotate your sets rather than reusing the exact same list on every post.

Hashtag Dos and Don'ts

Do

  • Mix popular, medium and niche hashtags in every set
  • Keep hashtags relevant to the actual content
  • Rotate your hashtag sets instead of repeating the same list
  • Research what your competitors and audience are using
  • Check each platform's recommended hashtag count

Don't

  • Stuff dozens of unrelated hashtags into one post
  • Use only the highest volume tags where you will never rank
  • Copy the exact same hashtag block on every single post
  • Ignore platform specific limits and norms
  • Use banned or flagged hashtags that can limit reach

Platform-Specific Hashtag Limits

Instagram: up to 30 hashtags per post or reel, with 8 to 15 recommended for balanced reach.

Twitter/X: no hard limit, but 1 to 2 hashtags per tweet is the widely recommended maximum for engagement.

TikTok: caption length limits mean 3 to 5 hashtags is typical, mixing broad and niche tags.

LinkedIn: 3 to 5 hashtags is the general best practice, placed at the end of the post text.

YouTube: up to 15 hashtags total, with only the first 3 displayed above the video title.
