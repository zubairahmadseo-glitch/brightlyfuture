Frequently Asked Questions

What does The Wow Style cover?

The Wow Style is a UK magazine covering haircuts, hairstyles, hair colour, grooming, fashion, beauty and celebrity style, starting with in-depth haircut guides.

Who writes and reviews your haircut guides?

Our guides are written by our editorial team and reviewed with working barbers and hairstylists. Each article shows its author, reviewer and last-updated date.

How do I choose a haircut for my face shape?

Start with our Face Shape Haircut Finder: identify whether your face is oval, round, square, heart, diamond or oblong, then use the matching guide for cuts that balance your proportions.

Can I write a guest post for The Wow Style?

Yes. We accept original, expert-led articles on hair, fashion and beauty. Read our Guest Post Guidelines and send your pitch through the Write for Us page.
