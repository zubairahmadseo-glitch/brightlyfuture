What Our AI Detector Analyses — Full Transparency

Unlike black-box tools, we show you exactly how we score your text.

🔢

Perplexity Score

Perplexity measures how predictable the word choices are. AI-generated text tends to choose the most statistically likely next word, resulting in low perplexity. Human writing is more varied and surprising, producing higher perplexity scores.

📈

Burstiness Score

Burstiness measures variation in sentence length and complexity. Humans naturally write with high burstiness — mixing short punchy sentences with longer complex ones. AI produces uniformly structured sentences with low burstiness.

📏

Sentence Uniformity

We measure how similar your sentences are in length and structure. AI models tend to generate sentences within a narrow length range (15–25 words). Human writers are more inconsistent — and that inconsistency is a strong authenticity signal.

📚

Vocabulary Richness

We calculate the ratio of unique words to total words. AI often recycles the same vocabulary throughout a piece, while humans draw from a broader lexicon. Higher vocabulary richness typically indicates human authorship.

Tips for Getting the Most Accurate AI Detection Results

Follow these best practices for reliable analysis.

1

Use at Least 50 Words — 150+ for Best Accuracy

Short snippets lack enough statistical signal for reliable analysis. Independent tests confirm that all AI detectors — including GPTZero and Copyleaks — perform significantly better on longer text samples.

2

Test Full Paragraphs, Not Bullet Points

Sentence fragments, bullet lists, and headings can skew detection results. Use complete paragraphs with natural sentence structure for the most accurate analysis.

3

Analyse Different Sections Separately

In long documents, different sections may have different authors or AI involvement. Test the introduction, body paragraphs, and conclusion separately for granular insight into each section.

4

No AI Detector Is 100% Accurate

Independent benchmarks show even top tools like GPTZero score 62–88% accuracy, and Copyleaks around 79%. Use AI detection as one signal alongside your own editorial judgement — never as the sole basis for consequential decisions.

5

Watch for Paraphrased AI Content

Text run through paraphrasing tools can fool most detectors — Copyleaks accuracy drops by 50% on humanised text. If you suspect paraphrasing, look for unnatural phrasing and vocabulary inconsistencies alongside our scores.
