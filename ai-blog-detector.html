Tips for Accurate AI Detection

Get the most reliable results from your scans.

1

Use at Least 50 Words

Short snippets lack enough statistical signal for reliable analysis. Paste at least 50 words — 150+ words gives the most accurate results.

2

Test Full Paragraphs, Not Fragments

Sentence fragments and bullet points can skew detection. Use complete paragraphs with natural sentence structure for best accuracy.

3

Check Multiple Sections Separately

In long documents, different sections may have different authors. Analyse introduction, body, and conclusion separately for granular insight.

4

Understand That No Detector Is 100% Accurate

AI detection is probabilistic, not definitive. Use results as one data point alongside your own editorial judgement — never as the sole basis for consequential decisions.

5

Beware of Paraphrasing Tools

Text that has been run through a paraphrasing or spinning tool can reduce detection accuracy. If you suspect this, look for unnatural phrasing and vocabulary inconsistencies.
