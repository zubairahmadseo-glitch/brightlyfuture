Cluster 2, sub-topic #4 · Researched 14 October 2026 · Focus keyword: **instagram bio ideas for business**
Business types (same six as #3, for consistency): cafe/restaurant, salon/beauty, online shop, fitness coach/gym, consultant/B2B service, blogger/creator.

## Method
Each competitor page was downloaded and read in full (headings, word count, claims). Pages that blocked the fetch are marked. Platform facts were checked against a primary or near-primary source; where sources disagree, the conflict is recorded.

## Gaps we can own
1. **Bios a small business can actually use.** Competitors show Nike and celebrities. We give 3 ready bios per business type (18 total), each under 150 characters with the count shown, labelled as examples to adapt.
2. **A 4-line formula** (what + for whom / proof / practical info / CTA), with each example mapped to it.
3. **Name field as the search keyword**, explained with business examples ("Rosa's | Leeds Bakery").
4. **Correct link rules**: 5 links since April 2023; which link goes first and why. Buffer and Metricool are wrong or outdated.
5. **Before/after rewrite** of a weak bio.
6. **Whole profile above the grid**: name, category, contact buttons, links, Highlights, pinned posts, in one short checklist.
7. **Measuring**: profile visits vs link taps and follows in Insights; change one line at a time.
8. **No fonts/fancy-text section**: special Unicode fonts are read badly by screen readers (accessibility angle, needs a source).
