## Our own data (experience / case study)
60 titles = top 10 YouTube search results for 6 business-style queries (sourdough, UK tax return, home workout, start a small business, Excel pivot tables, edit YouTube videos), fetched 17 Oct 2026 (UK, English):
- Median length **55 characters**; range 26–100; **18 of 60 (30%) over 70**.
- **56 of 60** contain the query's core term; **47 of 60** have it in the first 40 characters.
- **37 of 60** use brackets, a colon, pipe or dash to add a second part ("(Step-by-Step)", "| Complete Beginner Guide").
- **34 of 60** name the audience or ease ("beginner", "first", "easy", "step-by-step").
- **16 of 60** include a year; **32 of 60** contain a number.
- Only **6 of 60** use emojis; **17 of 60** have a word in ALL CAPS.
Caveat to state in article: one country, one day, search only; shows what ranks, not what caused it.

No BrightlyFuture YouTube channel experience exists → don't claim any. Examples will be labelled illustrations or taken from the sample.
