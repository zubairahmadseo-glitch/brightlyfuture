Cluster 2, sub-topic #5 · Researched 14 October 2026 · Focus keyword: **social media character limits**
Merged here: Twitter/X character limit (cluster 2 sheet #5) and cluster 1 #15 Character Count Guide.

## Search intent
Lookup. People want the number for a field before they post, then whether their text will be cut off, then what counts (spaces, emojis, links). Writers, social media managers, small business owners posting to several platforms. Not "how to write a caption" (owned by #2, #4, #6).

## Gaps we can own
1. **Every number sourced and dated**; unclear ones flagged honestly (TikTok app vs API, Facebook).
2. **Two numbers per field: the hard limit and what people see before "more"**, as approximate where unofficial.
3. **Business-relevant fields only** (post/caption, bio/profile, title, hashtags), not Discord/Twitch/WhatsApp.
4. **What counts as a character**: spaces, line breaks, emojis (can count as 2), links (X counts 23).
5. **What happens when you're over** (post button disabled / text cut / write an article on LinkedIn / Threads attachment / X Premium).
6. **Cross-posting one message**: write to the shortest limit you're posting to; first line carries it.
7. **Links out to our how-to articles** instead of repeating advice (zero overlap).
