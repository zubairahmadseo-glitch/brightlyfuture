# Keyword & Research Report – AI Paraphrasing vs Rewriting

Researched 27 September 2026. Search results pulled via web search (not a paid keyword tool), so no search-volume numbers are claimed here.

## Search intent
Informational. The reader wants to know whether paraphrasing and rewriting are the same thing, which one to use, and whether either counts as plagiarism or gets penalised by Google.

**One-line intent:** "Tell me the real difference, show me an example, and tell me which one is safe to use for my content."
