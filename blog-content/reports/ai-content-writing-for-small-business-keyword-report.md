## Social / community research: what was and wasn't possible
- **Reddit:** direct access and site-restricted search both returned no threads (Reddit blocks automated access). No Reddit quotes are used.
- **YouTube:** titles and dates were accessible, transcripts were not. Recent video themes (Mar–Aug 2026) focus on "what's actually working vs hype", solopreneur content systems and AI search visibility. These shaped the angle (practical, measured) but nothing is quoted.
- **Your own experience** (Claude for writing with business instructions; ChatGPT for images; Search Console 77 → 164 → 970 clicks) is the first-hand layer.
