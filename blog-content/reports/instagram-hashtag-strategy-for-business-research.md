# Research: Instagram Hashtag Strategy for Business
Cluster 2, sub-topic #3 · Researched 14 October 2026 · Focus keyword: **instagram hashtag strategy for business**
Business types covered (approved): cafe/restaurant, salon/beauty, online shop, fitness coach/gym, consultant/B2B, blogger/creator.

## The fact everything rests on
Instagram limits hashtags to **five per post or Reel**. Instagram's own wording, quoted by [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/) (Dec 2025): "Instagram will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five." Earlier tests limited some users to three. Several competitor pages still say 30 (Buffer, Sprout list counts, Beplan 5–15, Metadatareactor 8–12): they are out of date. Mosseri has said repeatedly that hashtags don't increase reach but tell Instagram what a post is about (same source).

## Search intent
Business owners who want more of the right people to find their posts and suspect their hashtags are wrong or pointless. They want: do hashtags still work, how many, which ones, how to find them, examples for their type of business, and how to tell if they work.

## Gaps we can own
1. **Correct 2026 rules** with Instagram's own wording; most pages still recommend 5–30.
2. **A method for choosing exactly five** (slot system). Competitors give size bands for 4–30 tags, never "which five".
3. **One worked five-tag set per business type** with reasoning, instead of 30–280-tag lists that can't be used any more.
4. **Buyer-intent vs peer tags** (Wild & Free's point) applied to every type: tags your customers search, not tags your competitors use.
5. **Honest measurement:** test sets over weeks; no invented benchmark numbers.
6. **Accessibility:** CamelCase hashtags for screen readers (none cover).
