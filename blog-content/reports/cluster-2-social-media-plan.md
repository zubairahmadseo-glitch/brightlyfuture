## Overlap rules while writing
- **Hashtags:** #3 Instagram and #9 TikTok must not repeat a general "what is a hashtag" section. #3 owns the 5-tag rule; #9 owns TikTok search and the FYP.
- **Instagram captions (#2) vs caption generator tool:** the article teaches writing; the tool page only explains the tool.
- **Instagram bio ideas (#4) vs bio generator tool:** article = examples and rules (150-character limit, one link); tool page = generator.
- **YouTube titles (#6) vs cluster 1 "How to Write Blog Headlines":** cover YouTube-only factors (title shown with the thumbnail, about 100-character limit, truncation in feeds). Link to the blog headline article for general formulas; don't repeat them.
- **YouTube titles (#6) vs YouTube tags (#7):** #7 says plainly that YouTube treats tags as minor; titles, thumbnails and descriptions belong to #6.
- **Character limits (#5):** check every number against each platform's own help centre on the day of writing. Sources disagree, especially for LinkedIn and TikTok.
- **AI tools (#11) vs cluster 1 "Best AI Blog Writing Tools":** #11 covers scheduling, captions, visuals and analytics tools only, not blog writers.
- **Content calendar (#10) vs pillar:** pillar mentions planning in one paragraph and links to #10.
