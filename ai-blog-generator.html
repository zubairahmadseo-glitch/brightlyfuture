5 Tips to Get Expert-Level Output

Small prompt tweaks that turn generic AI drafts into publish-ready articles.

1

Be specific with your topic

Instead of "coffee," write "how to brew pour-over coffee at home for beginners." Specificity narrows the AI's focus and produces sharper output.

2

Always include a focus keyword

Even one strong keyword tells the AI what to optimize around. Add 2–3 LSI keywords in the secondary field for better on-page SEO.

3

Match tone to your audience

A B2B SaaS blog needs "Authoritative." A lifestyle blog wins with "Conversational." Wrong tone kills engagement, no matter how good the content is.

4

Turn on Humanize Mode + Avoid AI Phrases

These two toggles dramatically reduce AI detection scores and remove clichés like "in today's digital landscape" and "delve into."

5

Always edit and fact-check

AI can hallucinate stats and quotes. Verify numbers, add a personal story or example, and update any dates before publishing.
