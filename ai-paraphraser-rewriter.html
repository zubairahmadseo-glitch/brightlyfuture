Tips for Getting the Best Paraphrasing Results

Follow these best practices for natural, high-quality rewrites.

1

Choose the Right Mode for Your Audience

Formal mode for business documents, Casual for blogs, Academic for research papers, Simple for general audiences. The right mode makes a bigger difference than any amount of manual editing.

2

Paraphrase Paragraphs, Not Fragments

Complete paragraphs give the algorithm more context to work with. Single sentences or bullet points produce less natural results — this is true for all paraphrasers including QuillBot.

3

Always Review and Edit the Output

No paraphrasing tool is perfect. Read through the output, check that the meaning is preserved, and make manual tweaks where needed. Independent tests show even QuillBot produces awkward phrasing at higher settings.

4

Use Multiple Modes on the Same Text

Try Standard first, then experiment with Creative or Formal. Each mode produces different variations — pick the one that sounds most natural for your specific use case.

5

Run Results Through an AI Detector

If you need your paraphrased text to pass AI detection tools, use our free AI Content Detector to check the output. Rewrite any flagged sentences manually for best results.
