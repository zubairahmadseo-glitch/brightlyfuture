**The biggest missing research: the ACSM 2026 Position Stand** (*Med Sci Sports Exerc*, April 2026, PubMed 41843416, 137 reviews). Only one competitor (Stronger app) cites it. Key points:
- Strength: at least 80% of 1RM, at least 2 sessions per week, 2–3 sets, key lifts early in the session.
- Hypertrophy: about **10 sets per muscle per week**. Load from low to high doesn't change growth as long as effort is high.
- Training to failure, machines vs free weights, and complex periodization don't consistently change results for the average adult.
- Source: https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/

This 2026 update is your strongest freshness and E-E-A-T signal.

## 7. Gap analysis → what to add

### 🔴 Must add
1. **ACSM 2026 block** (AEO): a 3-row table for strength / hypertrophy / power, straight from the position stand.
2. **"Why am I getting stronger but not bigger?"** as an H2: neural adaptation and skill, low weekly volume, sets stopped far from failure. Give a fix checklist.
3. **Similarities section:** both use progressive overload, both build some of each, both improve bone, joint and metabolic health.
4. **Benefits and drawbacks table** (Fitbod, BarBend, Crunch, ProSupps, WH all have one).
5. **Weekly volume by goal and level table:**
   - Strength: 2–3 sets per session × 2–3 sessions per week (ACSM).
   - Hypertrophy: about 10 sets starting point, up to 20 for advanced lifters. Use Pelland et al. 2025 (*Sports Med*) for diminishing returns.
6. **Sample 4-day hybrid week (PHUL-style):** sets × reps × % of 1RM × rest for each exercise.
7. **Sarcoplasmic vs myofibrillar:** one paragraph saying the evidence for "sarcoplasmic-only" growth is weak.

### 🟡 Differentiators
8. **Rep-range spectrum graphic:** 1–3 / 3–6 / 6–12 / 12–20 / 20–30, with what each one mainly builds.
9. **"Which should you pick?" decision table:**
   - Goal: look / lift / sport / health / over 50 / fat loss
   - Map each goal → recommended split
10. **Bone density and older adults:** Crunch cites heavier loads (80–85% 1RM) for bone. Cross-link to /reps-and-sets-for-adults-over-50/.
11. **How to track progress:**
    - Strength: e1RM, top-set weight.
    - Hypertrophy: tape measure, photos, reps at a fixed load.
12. **How long until results:** strength rises in 2–4 weeks (mostly neural); visible size takes 8–12 weeks. Find a citation before stating the numbers.
13. **Strength sports vs bodybuilding** in one table: powerlifting, weightlifting, strongman, bodybuilding.

### 🟢 Gaps nobody covers well (your edge)
14. **"Is 3x10 / 5x5 hypertrophy or strength?"** quick answer. Link to /what-does-3x10-4x12-or-5x5-mean-workout/.
15. **Specificity data with a source:** Haugen 2023 shows each equipment type improves its own test most. That turns your "strength is movement-specific" claim into evidence.
16. **Currier et al. 2023 (BJSM):**
    - 178 strength studies and 119 hypertrophy studies.
    - Best strength prescription: higher load, multiple sets, 3x/week.
    - Best hypertrophy prescription: higher load, multiple sets, 2x/week.
    - Every prescription beat no training.
    - Source: https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/18/1211
